FORMULA

Assumptions

Required: k,ρ,c_p>0 for the same isotropic medium state. Phase change, temperature-dependent integration and automatic multilayer homogenization are excluded.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
